## About
**Empowering & Scaling Nature-Positive Agriculture**
The transition to sustainable land management requires robust tools to measure and finance ecological outcomes. The ECOSYS Natural Capital Solutions (NCS) platform, powered by EOX, acts as a foundational digital Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (dMRV) hub. Designed for the Green Transition, it bridges the gap between Earth Observation (EO) data, ecological science, real-world farming systems, and financial markets. By translating complex environmental signals into transparent, verifiable metrics, the platform empowers stakeholders to develop, evaluate, and scale biodiversity and carbon farming activities effectively.

## The Context & Challenge
**Bridging the Gap Between Ecology and Capital**
Despite growing regulatory pressures—such as the Nature Restoration Law (NRL), the Common Agricultural Policy (CAP), and Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directives (CSRD)—financial markets and agricultural sectors have historically lacked a unified way to quantify nature risk and ecological impact. The challenge lies in moving from abstract biodiversity goals to tangible, compliance-ready assets. Without a reliable framework to establish baselines, evaluate risk, and monitor long-term impact at scale, unlocking financing for farmers who actively protect soil health, water resources, and biodiversity remains difficult.

## The Methodology
**Earth Observation Meets Ground-Truth Ecology**
To make the ecological outcomes of sustainable land management measurable, ECOSYS employs a science-led methodology that operates consistently from the individual parcel level up to national portfolios.

Data Integration: The framework fuses satellite-based Earth Observation signals with official geospatial datasets (such as LPIS/IACS, GSA, and soil maps).

The BCFI Metric: At the core of the methodology is the Biodiversity & Carbon Farming Index (BCFI). This index quantifies conservation values—encompassing biodiversity, soil organic carbon, and water metrics—into decision-ready scores.

True Value Accounting: Using the formula Portfolio Total Expected Impact Value (TEIV) = BCFI% × hectares × average ecosystem-service value, the platform calculates a comparative "book value." This represents true ecological impact and risk-adjusted financial eligibility rather than direct market pricing.

## Stakeholders & Applications
**Scalable Solutions for the Green Transition**
The NCS collection provides tailored workspace applications designed to turn biodiversity risks into scalable assets across diverse sectors:

Project Developers & NGOs: Rapid landscape screening and parcel analytics establish ecological baselines, enabling the design of Nature-Based Solutions with verified impact metrics and investor-ready reporting.

Investors & (Nature) Finance: Portfolio-level analytics offer risk-adjusted valuations and compliance-ready evidence, allowing financial institutions to identify "Green Premium" fields, de-risk investments, and confidently deploy capital.

Corporate Supply Chains: Supply chain managers can monitor Scope 3 biodiversity risks and verify regenerative sourcing claims across vast agricultural networks, avoiding lengthy on-the-ground verification processes.
